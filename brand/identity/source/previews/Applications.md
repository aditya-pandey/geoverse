# Applications

The brand in three places people meet it first.

**Share card** (1200 × 630, also the Open Graph image):
- World confetti on `space`, with the night wordmark top-left.
- The result in `display-xl`, ringed by one teacher's circle.
- The dare underneath in `hand`.

**Loading screen:** the two worlds turning slowly on `space`, with "Spinning up the world…" in `body` and `ink-muted`. It cross-fades to the globe in `--t-slow`.

**Home-screen icon:** the full-bleed app icon, two worlds and a marigold sparkle on `space`. The platform rounds the corners.

These are built from the bundle (`data-bh-*` placeholders plus `Bhoogol.hydrate()`), so they stay in sync with the logo files.
