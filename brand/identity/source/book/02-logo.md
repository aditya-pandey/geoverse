# Logo

{{img:logo-night}}

## The idea

**The two O's are two worlds.** "bhoogol" is set in round, heavy, friendly letters. The double *oo* in the middle becomes two little planets: the Americas on the left in mint, and Afro-Eurasia on the right in marigold. India's peninsula and Australia are both there if you look closely. The idea fits in one sentence, and it reads at a glance as both the name and the subject.

## Construction

- **Letters:** drawn from circles and straight stems, not typed. The stem is 30 units, the x-height 104, and the bowls are rings of radius 54 with 24-unit counters. Free ends are round, like a pencil or a marble.
- **Worlds:** each is a filled bowl of the same radius 54. The worlds are solid, so they read a little heavier than the letters. That's intended: they are the point.
- **Land:** soft shapes drawn as B-splines and kept inside the rim. They suggest the continents without tracing them.
- **Devanagari भूगोल:** drawn to match the Latin, with the same round ends and monoline weight, a 26-unit stroke and a 24-unit headline. Set beside the Latin at the same scale, the strokes match.

## Colour on the logo

- Worlds always have `ocean` sea, `mint` west and `marigold` east. Never recolour them.
- Letters are `ink` on day (`space`, #12133A) and `chalk` on night (#F7F5EF).
- In one colour, the land is knocked out of the worlds, so the background shows through.
- In the night bilingual lockup, भूगोल may take `marigold`.

## Clear space and size

- **Clear space:** keep at least **half a world** (the radius of one O) clear on every side. It scales with the logo.
- **Minimum size:** the wordmark at **96 px** wide on screens or 24 mm in print. Below that, use the single world.
- **Bilingual lockup:** 160 px wide minimum.
- **Favicon:** the single world reads at 16 px; the two-world symbol needs 32 px.

## Please don't

- Retype the name in a font, or swap the worlds for emoji 🌍.
- Recolour, rotate or animate the land shapes.
- Put the colour wordmark on `ocean` blue, where the worlds disappear.
- Add outlines, shadows, glows or gradients.
- Stretch, squash or re-space the letters.
- Use the one-colour version when colour is available.
