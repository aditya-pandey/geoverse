# Graphic language

Four things carry the brand beyond the logo. Use one or two per screen, not all four.

## 1 · Teacher's marks

{{img:marks}}

These are a quick loop, an underline, an arrow, a tick and a sparkle, drawn like a confident pencil stroke: thick in the middle, tapering at the ends. They come from "mark it on the outline map".

- **Colour:** marks are always `marigold`. On day backgrounds, where marigold is light, put the mark behind a darker element or use `highlight-ink`.
- **One per view.** A mark points at the single thing that matters: the answer, the place, the new feature.
- **Never decoration:** a mark always marks something.
- **Notes:** a mark may carry a two-to-five-word note in `hand` (Kalam) beside it.
- **Motion:** marks draw on in one stroke (240 ms, `settle` easing) and never loop.
- **Files:** `marker-circle`, `marker-underline`, `marker-arrow`, `marker-tick` and `marker-sparkle` in the Marks group, or `Bhoogol.mark(kind)` inline.

## 2 · Little worlds

{{img:worlds}}

Worlds are the logo's planets, scaled up and given a night side: a flat crescent of `space` at 32% over the side away from the light. The light always comes from the upper left.

- Use them for empty states, loading screens and section headers.
- They're always round, always flat, with no texture. The land is the soft logo shapes, never a traced map.
- The moon is the only other body in the cast.

## 3 · Kanche (marbles)

These are glass marbles with a twisted vane, a glassy highlight and a night side. In the product, **you collect a marble** for finishing a game or a tour:

- blue for oceans and rivers
- mint for countries
- marigold for ranges and wonders

A row of marbles shows progress at a glance and feels like a pocketful of kanche.

## 4 · World confetti

{{img:pattern}}

The pattern is a calm scatter of worlds, a moon, marbles, sparkles and star dots on `space`. Use it for share cards, splash screens and merch backs. Keep it calm: a few elements per tile, generous gaps, nothing touching the edge mid-shape.

## Shapes

Corners are round: `radius-pill` for anything you press, `radius-m` for cards and `radius-l` for sheets. Discs and pills are the only containers. There are no hexagons, no sharp angles and no coloured left-border cards.
