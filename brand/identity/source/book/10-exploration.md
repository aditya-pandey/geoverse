# How we got here

{{img:explore}}

Round 1 (a tile with a sunbeam, a patchwork globe, and a globe on a tilted axis) was clean but cold. All three marks were about the *object*, a globe, rather than the feeling of playing with one.

Round 2 started from words instead: *bhoo + gol*, the classroom globe, kanche, the Blue Marble, "mark it on the outline map". Three ideas came out of it:

1. **Kancha:** the Earth as a cat's-eye marble. It's lovely, but at small sizes its swirl reads as a swoosh. It lives on as **the marbles you collect**.
2. **Two worlds:** the *oo* in the name as two little planets. It reads instantly as the name and the subject, works at 16px, and is unlike anything else in the category. **This is the logo.**
3. **Circle it:** a pencil loop around a place. It's charming but busy as a logo. It lives on as **the teacher's marks**.

So nothing was wasted: one idea became the logo and the other two became the system around it.
