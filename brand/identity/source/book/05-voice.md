# Voice

**A curious friend who knows a lot, not a teacher with a red pen.** Short sentences. Real facts. A light touch of humour when something goes right, and plain help when something goes wrong.

## Principles

- **Lead with the world, not the app.** "Bahrain is joined to Saudi Arabia by a 25 km causeway." beats "Did you know? Fun fact!"
- **Use "you".** "We" is for the team and appears rarely.
- **Be specific.** Use names, numbers and places. A real fact is the best decoration.
- **Celebrate briefly, help clearly.** One word for a win, one sentence for a fix.
- **Hindi as flavour, not code.** An occasional Hindi word is welcome where its meaning is obvious ("Shabash!" after a streak). Never hide an instruction behind one.
- **No decorative emoji.** Flags and the little-world glyph are content. 🚀🎉✨ are not.

## Examples

| Moment | Write | Not |
|---|---|---|
| Primary button | Fly there | Click here to navigate |
| Quiz, correct | Shabash! That's Bolivia. | 🎉🎉 Correct answer!!! |
| Quiz, wrong | Not quite. That's Paraguay, and Bolivia is just north. | Wrong! Try again. |
| Streak | 5 in a row | Amazing streak unlocked! |
| Empty search | No place called "Atlantis". Try a country, river or range. | No results found. |
| 3D view paused | Your device paused the globe. Bringing it back… | ⏸️ The device paused the 3D view, restoring… |
| No WebGL | This device can't draw the 3D globe. The 2D map still works. | Error: WEBGL_UNAVAILABLE |
| Loading | Spinning up the world… | Loading, please wait |
| Share card | I found 9 of 10 countries on Bhoogol. Can you? | Check out my score!!! |

## Casing and punctuation

- Use sentence case.
- One exclamation mark per screen, at most, and only for a win.
- Use the ellipsis (…) only for real waiting.
- Use numerals for numbers ("3 rivers"), except at the start of a sentence.
