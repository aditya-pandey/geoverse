# Worlds

The brand's illustration cast. Each is drawn flat, lit from the upper left, with a night side of `space` at 32%.

- `world-west.svg`: the Americas, with mint land.
- `world-east.svg`: Afro-Eurasia, India and Australia, with marigold land.
- `moon.svg`: the only other body in the cast.
- `kancha-blue.svg`, `kancha-mint.svg`, `kancha-marigold.svg`: the collectible marbles (blue for oceans and rivers, mint for countries, marigold for ranges and wonders).
- `pattern-confetti.svg`: a 512 × 512 tile of world confetti on `space`. It repeats seamlessly only in its calm areas; use it as a backdrop and don't tile it tightly.
