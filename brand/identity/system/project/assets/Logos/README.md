# Logos

These are the drawn logo files. Don't retype the name in a font.

- `bhoogol-wordmark-day.svg`: letters in `space` (#12133A) and colour worlds, for `chalk` and light grounds.
- `bhoogol-wordmark-night.svg`: letters in `chalk` (#F7F5EF) and colour worlds, for `space` and dark grounds. It's invisible on white.
- `bhoogol-wordmark-black.svg` and `bhoogol-wordmark-white.svg`: one ink (#000000 or #FFFFFF), with the land knocked out of the worlds.
- `bhoogol-devanagari-day.svg` and `bhoogol-devanagari-night.svg`: भूगोल in `space` or `chalk`.
- `bhoogol-bilingual-day.svg` and `bhoogol-bilingual-night.svg`: Latin above Devanagari. The night version sets भूगोल in `marigold`.
- `bhoogol-symbol.svg`: the two worlds. `-black` and `-white` are one ink with the land knocked out.
- `bhoogol-world.svg`: the single eastern world, for the favicon and anything under 32px.
- `bhoogol-app-icon.svg`: the full-bleed app icon, two worlds on `space` with a marigold sparkle.
