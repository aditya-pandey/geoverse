What we heard

The brief

A free 3D globe for learning geography, on phones and desktops. Bhoo means Earth and gol means round; together they make the everyday Hindi word for geography. It should feel curious, calm and playful, like a well-made desk globe, and never childish.

curiousplayfulcalmsmoothwarmclever

Success: unlike grid globes and map pins, recognisable at 16 px, works in one colour on navy and on white, and pairs with a Devanagari wordmark.

Concept A

Pale Blue Dot

One small blue dot caught in a beam of sunlight, the way Voyager saw the Earth.

  • Carries the Carl Sagan story you were drawn to, with no globe cliché
  • The app can open the same way: a dot in a sunbeam that grows into the globe
  • A strong app icon; the favicon has its own drawing with a larger dot
  • The beam's 15° angle is repeated in the cut tops of the b, h and l, and a matching custom-drawn भूगोल completes the bilingual lockup

Recommendation & next steps

Our recommendation

Pale Blue Dot. It is the most ownable of the three, it tells the Sagan story in one image, it makes the strongest app icon, and it gives the app a ready-made opening animation.

  • Pick a direction, or tell me what you like in each
  • Have a fluent reader check the custom भूगोल letterforms
  • Wire the icons and logo into the app, and run a trademark search
