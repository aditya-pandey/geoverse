    About Bhoogol

    A 3D globe for learning geography, built on open data and open-source software. Thank you to everyone below.

    Maps & imagery

    • Country, ocean, range, lake & river shapes — Natural Earth (public domain), via world-atlas (ISC)
    • Terrain colours — derived from NASA's Blue Marble (public domain)
    • Relief shading — GEBCO_08 elevation map from NASA Earth Observatory (public domain)
    • India's official outline & states — Survey of India boundaries via DataMeet (MIT)
    • States & provinces — geoBoundaries (gbOpen; licence per boundary) — Runfola et al. (2020), PLOS ONE

    Facts & numbers

    • Country facts — world-countries by Mohammed Le Doze (ODbL 1.0)
    • State lists — countries-states-cities-database (ODbL 1.0)
    • Population, GDP & life expectancy — World Bank Open Data (CC BY 4.0)
    • Stories & photos — Wikipedia (CC BY-SA); each summary links to its article
    • Flags — flagcdn.com
    • 3D animal & plant models — artists on Sketchfab; each model shows its title, author and licence

    Software

    • three.js (MIT) · d3 (ISC) · topojson-client (ISC) · earcut (ISC) · canvas-confetti (ISC)
    • Fonts — Baloo 2, Mukta, Kalam & JetBrains Mono via Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License)

    Map borders show how the source data draws them; India is shown with its official Survey of India boundary.
